# Dimensions

## Overworld

The Overworld is the primary dimension in Minecraft.

Players normally begin their world in the Overworld.

It contains common terrain such as forests, plains, mountains, oceans, deserts, and caves.

## Nether

The Nether is a dangerous dimension containing unique terrain, mobs, structures, and resources.

Players enter the Nether through an activated Nether portal.

Important Nether resources include Nether quartz, glowstone, ancient debris, and Netherite-related materials.

## Nether Portal

A Nether portal connects the Overworld and Nether.

The minimum portal frame can be constructed using ten obsidian blocks by leaving the four corners empty.

The portal is activated by creating fire inside the obsidian frame.

## End

The End is a separate dimension associated with the Ender Dragon.

Players can reach the End through an End portal found in a stronghold.

The End contains End cities and other unique structures and resources.

## Ender Dragon

The Ender Dragon is the main boss associated with the End dimension.

Defeating the Ender Dragon provides access to the dragon's experience reward and enables access to the End gateway system.

## Netherite

Netherite is a high-tier material obtained using resources found in the Nether.

Ancient debris can be mined in the Nether and processed into materials used for Netherite equipment.

Netherite equipment is generally more durable than its diamond equivalents.
