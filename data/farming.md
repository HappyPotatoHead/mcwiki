# Farming

## Wheat Farm

Wheat can be grown by planting wheat seeds on farmland.

Farmland can be created by using a hoe on suitable dirt-based blocks.

Crops need appropriate conditions to grow.

Water can hydrate farmland and support crop growth nearby.

## Carrot Farm

Carrots can be planted directly on farmland.

Carrots can be harvested and replanted to continue producing crops.

Carrots can also be used as food for players and villagers.

## Potato Farm

Potatoes can be planted on farmland.

Harvesting a mature potato crop produces potatoes that can be replanted.

Potatoes can also be cooked to produce baked potatoes.

## Sugar Cane

Sugar cane can be planted on suitable blocks adjacent to water.

It grows vertically and can be harvested without removing the bottom block.

Sugar cane is useful for crafting paper.

## Automatic Farms

Automatic farms use Minecraft mechanics and redstone components to reduce or eliminate manual harvesting.

Different farms use different mechanics.

A farm design should account for the required blocks, available space, collection system, and game mechanics involved.

## Villager Farming

Villagers can participate in crop farming when they have the appropriate profession.

Farmer villagers can plant and harvest certain crops.

Villager-based farms can use this behaviour to automate parts of a farming system.

## Crop Growth

Crop growth is affected by the surrounding environment and game mechanics.

Keeping farmland hydrated and providing suitable light conditions can help crops grow.

Minecraft's random tick system controls many natural growth processes.
