# Enchantments

## Sharpness

Sharpness increases the damage dealt by a weapon.

It can be applied to swords and axes.

## Protection

Protection reduces damage taken from many sources.

It can be applied to armour pieces.

## Unbreaking

Unbreaking increases an item's effective durability by giving uses a chance not to consume durability.

It can be applied to many durable items, including weapons, tools, and armour.

## Mending

Mending uses experience orbs to repair an item with the Mending enchantment instead of adding the experience directly to the player's experience bar.

Mending can be applied to many durable items.

## Fortune

Fortune increases the amount or likelihood of certain item drops when mining or harvesting applicable blocks.

It can be applied to tools such as pickaxes, shovels, and axes.

## Silk Touch

Silk Touch allows certain blocks to be obtained in their original form instead of producing their usual drops.

It is commonly useful when mining blocks such as glass or certain ore blocks.

## Efficiency

Efficiency increases the speed at which a tool performs its applicable actions.

It can be applied to tools such as pickaxes, axes, shovels, and hoes.

## Feather Falling

Feather Falling reduces fall damage.

It can be applied to boots.

## Looting

Looting increases certain mob drops when a mob is killed using a weapon with the enchantment.

It can be applied to swords.

## Infinity

Infinity allows a bow to fire normal arrows without consuming them, provided the player has at least one normal arrow in their inventory.

Infinity does not apply to tipped arrows or spectral arrows.

## Enchanting Compatibility

Not every enchantment can be combined with every other enchantment.

Some enchantments are mutually exclusive. For example, a tool generally cannot have both Silk Touch and Fortune at the same time.
