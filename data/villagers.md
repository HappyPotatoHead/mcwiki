# Villagers

## Villager Breeding

Villagers can breed when the required conditions are satisfied.

Villagers need access to valid beds and must have enough food to become willing to breed.

Food such as bread, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot can be used to make villagers willing to breed.

## Villager Jobs

Villagers can obtain professions when they claim an appropriate job site block.

Examples include:

- Librarian: Lectern
- Farmer: Composter
- Armorer: Blast Furnace
- Toolsmith: Smithing Table
- Weaponsmith: Grindstone
- Cleric: Brewing Stand
- Fisherman: Barrel
- Fletcher: Fletching Table

An unemployed villager can claim an available job site block.

## Librarian

A librarian is a villager whose profession is associated with a lectern.

Librarians can offer enchanted books as trades.

Players often use librarian villagers when trying to obtain particular enchanted books.

## Farmer

A farmer uses a composter as their job site block.

Farmers can plant and harvest crops.

Farmers can also share food with other villagers, which can help support villager breeding.

## Villager Trading

Villagers can trade items with players.

Trading can increase a villager's experience and eventually cause the villager to level up.

Villager trades vary depending on the villager's profession and level.

## Villager Zombies

Zombie villagers are hostile mobs that can be cured.

A zombie villager can be converted back into a normal villager using the appropriate curing process.

Curing a zombie villager can provide benefits to the player's future trades with that villager.

## Villager Problems

If villagers are not behaving as expected, useful things to check include:

- Whether they can access their beds
- Whether they have enough food
- Whether beds are available
- Whether job site blocks are accessible
- Whether another villager has already claimed a nearby job site
